# HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau Project

### Transforming Employee Data into Meaningful HR Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Tableau. It focuses on analyzing employee demographics, workforce distribution, job satisfaction, and employee attrition.

The dashboard provides an overview of important HR metrics and helps identify patterns across departments, gender, age groups, and educational backgrounds.

Through interactive filters and visualizations, this project aims to make HR data easier to understand and support data-driven workforce decisions.

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and workforce distribution.
- Understand attrition patterns across different genders.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Explore employee distribution across different age groups.
- Analyze job satisfaction across various job roles.
- Examine attrition based on educational backgrounds.
- Understand attrition patterns across gender and age groups.

##  Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1. Attrition by Gender
- Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.
- Male employees account for 150 attrition cases.
- Female employees account for 87 attrition cases.

### 2. Department-wise Attrition
Analyzes employee attrition across three major departments:

- Research & Development: 133 employees
- Sales: 92 employees
- Human Resources: 12 employees

### 3. Employee Distribution by Age Group
- Visualizes workforce distribution across different age groups.
- Highlights the concentration of employees in different age categories.
- The age group of 33 has the highest employee count, with 213 employees.

### 4. Job Satisfaction Analysis
- Examines job satisfaction ratings across different job roles.
- Uses a heatmap to compare satisfaction levels.
- Sales Executives represent the largest employee group, with 326 employees.

### 5. Education Field-wise Attrition
- Analyzes employee attrition across different educational backgrounds.
- Helps identify education fields with higher attrition counts.

### 6. Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group
- Compares male and female attrition patterns across different age groups.
- Uses donut charts for visual comparison.
